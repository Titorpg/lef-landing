## Quién es LEF

**LEF — Learn English Fluently**

> In LEF, we believe that learning English goes beyond memorizing grammar rules or following traditional methods. We provide a dynamic, communicative and contextualized learning experience to help students use English confidently in real-life situations.

Claim corto: *"Reach your goals and master English by using it in real life."*

## Qué es LEF

LEF is an online structured English learning program designed for real communicative competence. It combines academic clarity, contextualized instruction, and measurable progress.

## El sistema de aprendizaje LEF (3 pilares)

1. **Communicative Focus** — English is used from day one.
2. **Contextualized Learning** — Language is taught through real situations.
3. **Continuous Progress** — Structured cycles ensure steady improvement.

## Qué ofrece LEF

1. Live Online Classes
2. Conversation Club
3. Exclusive LEF Learning Materials
4. Academic Platform (actualmente Google Classroom — en la landing nueva, referirse de forma neutral como "plataforma académica" ya que se planea unificar esto)
5. Mid-Cycle Tutoring Sessions
6. Continuous Academic Support

## Evaluación y progreso

LEF follows a formative assessment model in which progress is monitored continuously through participation and language performance. At the end of each cycle, students take a **Knowledge Validation Exam** to verify the achievement of learning objectives. This exam is not punitive and does not determine advancement to the next level; it is designed to support reflection, progress awareness, and learning without unnecessary pressure.

## Compromiso del estudiante

LEF students are expected to:
1. Participate actively in every session
2. Maintain respectful communication
3. Attend classes consistently
4. Complete assigned tasks
5. Take responsibility for their learning

**Structured Learning. Real Communication. Continuous Growth.**
